Know who needs help before the exam does

LearniQ sits on top of your college ERP. It reads attendance, internal marks and assignments, flags students who may need support, and explains why.

How it works

  1. Export from the ERPDownload attendance, internal marks and assignment data as a CSV file.
  2. Upload to LearniQThe system checks the file and shows clear errors if a row is wrong.
  3. Features are calculatedAttendance percentage, average marks, marks trend and submission rate.
  4. The model predicts riskEach student gets a risk level and a risk score.
  5. Teachers act earlyThe dashboard shows reasons and suggested actions, and teachers log the support they give.
