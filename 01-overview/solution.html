Solution

An add-on layer that sits on top of any existing college ERP. It does not replace the ERP.

How it works

  1. Export attendance, internal marks and assignment data from the ERP as a CSV file.
  2. Upload the CSV to LearniQ. The system checks and cleans the data.
  3. Features are calculated: attendance percentage, average marks, marks trend and assignment submission rate.
  4. A machine learning model predicts each student's risk.
  5. The dashboard shows the risk level, the reasons and suggested actions.

Inputs and outputs

InputOutput
Attendance percentageRisk level and risk score
Internal marks across testsReasons, for example "attendance 58%, marks fell in the last 3 tests"
Assignment submission rate and scoresSuggested actions for the teacher and student

What makes it different

  • Add-on, not replacement: works with the ERP the college already has.
  • Explainable: shows why a student is at risk, not only that they are.
  • Trend-based: detects falling marks before the next result.
  • Student view: students see their own risk and suggestions.
  • Action tracking: teachers log support given and see whether risk reduced.
The model supports faculty decisions. It does not replace them, and a teacher always makes the final call.
