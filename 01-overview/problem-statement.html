Problem statement

Problem AI-03, Student Performance Prediction.

SummaryThis page describes why students may need academic support before exams and the early-warning output LearniQ is intended to provide.

The problem

Students may not recognise declining academic performance until examinations. Attendance drops, internal marks slip and assignments go missing, but nobody sees the pattern early enough to act.

The task

Use sample attendance, internal marks and assignment scores to identify students who may need academic support.

Expected output

OutputDescription
Risk levelA risk level for every student: High, Medium or Low.
Teacher dashboardA list of students who may need academic support.
Early warningA warning before exams, not after results.

Why existing tools are not enough

TodayGap
College ERP stores attendance, marks, assignments and feesIt records data but does not warn when performance falls.
Faculty report marks after internalsThe warning arrives after the result, when it is too late to change it.
Each faculty member sees one subjectNobody sees the combined pattern across subjects.
Students see numbers onlyThey do not know their risk or what to improve.

Common questions

We already have an ERP. Why this?

The ERP stores data but does not warn. LearniQ is an add-on: export a CSV and it gives risk levels.

Faculty already tell us who failed. Why is this different?

Faculty report after results. LearniQ flags risk before results and looks across all subjects together.

How accurate is it?

No model is 100% accurate. It focuses on Recall so at-risk students are not missed, and accuracy is measured on the institute's own data during the pilot.

What if a prediction is wrong?

The teacher makes the final decision. The system provides a priority list with reasons.
